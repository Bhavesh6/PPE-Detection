The Gap Is at the Gate

Sites don't fail because nobody issued the equipment. They fail because nobody is watching the moment a worker walks in without it.

Safety inspection is still mostly a supervisor walking the site checking for hard hats and harnesses — an approach industry research describes as labour-intensive, time-consuming and susceptible to human error. Workers who do carry their PPE often remove it temporarily in hazardous areas, and those moments are the hardest of all to catch by observation.

A checkpoint doesn't get tired, doesn't look away, and doesn't wave anyone through because it's the end of a shift.

Checks What the Rules Already Require

The checkpoint isn't enforcing an invented policy. Helmet and reflective jacket are baseline statutory PPE on Indian sites — this simply verifies them at the entrance.

Statutory Baseline

The BOCW Act, Factories Act and Occupational Safety Code oblige employers to supply PPE free of cost. Minimum kit on site: helmet, safety shoes and reflective jacket.

ISI-Certified Equipment

PPE must be ISI-certified to BIS standards and replaced once damaged. Verification at the gate is what turns that requirement into a daily habit.

Evidence After the Fact

DGMS and factory inspectorates ask what controls were in place. A timestamped record of every entry decision answers that question with data, not recollection.

How It Works

Four steps, running continuously, frame by frame.

01

Capture

Webcam or IP camera input, from any device or browser. No special hardware required.

02

Detect

YOLOv8 checks the frame for person, hardhat and safety vest — and flags each one that's missing.

03

Evaluate

Checks which PPE items are present against what's required, and classifies the frame as compliant or a violation.

04

Respond

Dashboard updates instantly, an audio alert fires, and the violation is logged with a timestamp.

Three Surfaces, One Record

The gate makes the call. The console proves it happened. Everyone sees the part that concerns them.

Gate Control

The live checkpoint. Camera feed, a per-item PPE checklist, and an unambiguous grant-or-deny ruling that names exactly what's missing.

  • Frame-by-frame verification
  • Spoken alert on refusal
  • Fullscreen kiosk mode

Compliance Console

The supervisor's view. Site-wide compliance rate, who is live at a checkpoint right now, and the full decision history for any individual.

  • Granted vs. denied per person
  • Live checkpoint status
  • Per-person detection log

Worker Records

What each person sees about themselves — their own compliance rate and every entry decision made about them, filterable and timestamped.

  • Filter granted / denied
  • Survives restarts
